A real orchard. Lovingly tended, freshly picked.

MangoBerryEstate has been a labour of love for four generations of our family. We proudly grow some of India's most cherished mango varieties — Langda, Dussheri, and Chausa — alongside juicy litchis that ripen in early June. Our kitchen garden also bursts with seasonal favourites: okra, tomatoes, brinjals, gourds, and tender leafy greens, all lovingly tended and ready for you to harvest.

When you book a slot, we set aside a basket just for your group. Our warm and friendly team will gladly guide you through the orchard rows, show you the ripest, sweetest fruits ready for picking, and weigh your harvest at the gate — so you can take the freshest produce straight home with you.
